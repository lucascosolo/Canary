# H-04: Terms read in a browser where WebFetch failed

Read 2026-11-04 by the orchestrating session in the local browser. Quotes are word for word from the
rendered page. Verdicts are the author's reading, not legal advice.

- **Cline Center Coup d'Etat Project v2.2.2.** https://databank.illinois.edu/datasets/IDB-7095878 has
  a License field reading "CC BY", with no version shown. Data citation given by the page: "Peyton,
  Buddy, Joseph Bajjalieh, Michael Martin, and Andrea Gerald. 2026. Cline Center Coup d'État Project
  Dataset. Cline Center for Advanced Social Research. V.2.2.2. February 17. University of Illinois
  Urbana-Champaign. doi: 10.13012/B2IDB-9651987_V10". Verdict: open.
- **Early Warning Project.** The site footer points to
  https://www.ushmm.org/copyright-and-legal-information/terms-of-use (updated May 9, 2025). "Visitors
  are encouraged to use this website for personal, educational, research, and other purposes in
  accordance with these Terms." On putting Content on a website: "Yes, as long as you comply with these
  Terms of Use." Required: cite the "United States Holocaust Memorial Museum" (with no abbreviations),
  link where possible, and do not imply endorsement. Forbidden without written consent: using Content
  "to train, fine-tune, or otherwise develop any machine learning or artificial intelligence models".
  The Museum may revoke permission. The 13 repositories at https://github.com/earlywarningproject
  declare no licence in the GitHub API. Verdict: display, for the unchanged published assessment.
- **Internet Archive.** https://archive.org/about/terms (dated 31 Dec 2014): access "is granted for
  scholarship and research purposes only", use must be "limited to noninfringing or fair use under
  copyright law", and "You agree not to interfere with the work of other users or Archive personnel,
  servers, or resources." The terms do not address the availability or save APIs. Verdict: links. Store
  the capture URL and not the content, check availability before saving, and keep saves low in volume.
- **V-Dem ERT.** https://www.v-dem.net/data/ert/ is not found, and the GitHub licence API returns 404
  for vdeminstitute/ERT. Remains unverified.
